# Premium Agency & Healthcare Digital Reference Notes

## Healthcare Web Agency — https://www.healthcarewebagency.com/

- Positions itself immediately as a healthcare specialist rather than a generic digital agency.
- Hero makes patient-oriented outcomes visible through a concise headline, a short set of standards, and a clear audit call-to-action.
- Navigation separates services, industries, portfolio, and contact pathways, which helps prospective medical practices self-qualify.
- Service framing combines website design, healthcare SEO, and Google Business Profile management under one patient-trust and visibility story.

## OHO Healthcare — https://www.oho.com/solutions-services/healthcare-internet-marketing

- Selected for research because it presents healthcare marketing as an integrated enterprise capability, rather than isolated marketing tasks.
- The GOTM redesign should follow this strategic integration principle while using an original visual identity, structure, copy system, colors, and typography.

## Work & Co — https://work.co/

- Uses a single concise promise about solving complex problems through design and technology, then proves its breadth through selected work, practice areas, outcomes, process, and insights.
- The landing page avoids a catalogue-style service pitch. It prioritizes a confident proposition, editorial whitespace, clear hierarchy, and evidence of sophisticated work.

## Instrument — https://www.instrument.com/

- Frames itself as a digital-first agency operating across brand, product, marketing, and technology, presenting those disciplines as a connected system rather than separate retainers.
- Establishes credibility with selective work and client recognition before moving into detailed service explanation.
- Uses a simple navigation model centred on Work, Services, About, and Contact, providing an enterprise-facing experience without an overloaded menu.

## Cardinal Digital Marketing — https://www.cardinaldigitalmarketing.com/healthcare-services/website-design/

- Positions high-growth healthcare groups around a scalable digital front door rather than a standalone website.
- Connects information architecture, location growth, patient journeys, technical performance, search readiness, content planning, and analytics into one build narrative.
- Explicitly addresses the multi-location challenge, showing that scalable design systems and centralized content structures are pivotal to larger healthcare organizations.

## Healthcare Success — https://healthcaresuccess.com/

- Uses an integrated-care narrative covering strategy, branding, websites, paid search, SEO, organic and paid social, content, analytics, and healthcare-specific operational complexity.
- Speaks directly to systems with multiple providers and offices, focusing on patient acquisition and measurable outcomes rather than isolated deliverables.

## Implications for GOTM

- Lead with the value of a unified digital ecosystem for physician groups and multi-location organizations: platform, provider data, local listings, search visibility, paid media, social content, and measurement.
- Place health systems and physician groups at the first decision point in the hero and primary navigation.
- Preserve local service offerings as a clearly labeled secondary service line with its current pricing, rather than allowing it to define the company brand.
- Build an original visual system around composed editorial layout, highly legible typography, carefully art-directed motion, and concrete proof of how every touchpoint connects; do not recreate any reference site's appearance.
- Organize GOTM's primary offer as a connected operating model: Digital Foundation, Discoverability, Demand, Reputation, and Intelligence. This gives sophisticated buyers a clear view of the entire system without presenting disconnected tactics.
