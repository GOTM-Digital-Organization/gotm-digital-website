## Idea A — "Dark Gold Authority"

**Design Movement:** Refined Dark Luxury / Editorial Finance

**Core Principles:**
1. Dark backgrounds with gold accents signal premium without pretension
2. Generous whitespace creates breathing room and authority
3. Serif display headings contrast with clean sans-serif body for editorial credibility
4. Asymmetric column layouts break the "template" feel

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: #0B0F18 (near-black navy) — communicates depth and expertise
- Gold: #F4A12E / #F9C46B — warmth, value, trust (matches flyer exactly)
- Text: #D8E0EE — readable, not harsh white
- Muted: #7A8599 — supporting text
- Accent cards: #141922 / #1C2333

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-width dark hero with centered gold crosshair logo + headline
- Off-center two-column sections alternating text/visual
- Horizontal rule dividers in gold at 30% opacity
- Sticky nav with transparent-to-solid scroll behavior

**Signature Elements:**
1. Gold crosshair/target motif (from logo) used as section dividers and bullet icons
2. Thin gold horizontal rules as section separators
3. "Honest Truth" callout boxes with gold left border

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states reveal gold underlines on links
- Cards lift with subtle shadow on hover
- Accordion sections animate open/close smoothly

**Animation:**
- Fade-up entrance on scroll for each section (60ms stagger)
- Gold shimmer on CTA button hover
- Number counters animate on scroll-into-view

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display 700/900 — authority and elegance
- Body: DM Sans 400/500/600 — modern, readable
- Labels: DM Sans 600, letter-spacing 4px, uppercase, gold

## CHOSEN APPROACH: Idea A — "Dark Gold Authority"

This directly matches the flyer's established brand identity (dark background, gold accents, Playfair Display + DM Sans), creating visual continuity between the flyer and website. The dark luxury aesthetic signals expertise without arrogance, and the gold accents reinforce the "value" messaging of honest, affordable pricing. The asymmetric layout and editorial typography prevent the "AI slop" centered-grid look.
